# Plan B Backlink Campaign

Date: 2026-07-09
Primary asset: https://aibeautystylist.com/research/virtual-makeup-privacy-index
Dataset: https://aibeautystylist.com/research/virtual-makeup-privacy-index-2026.csv

## Objective

Build 100 safer backlinks by creating an editorial reason to cite AI Beauty Stylist, then pitching the asset to people who already cover beauty technology, virtual try-on, AI photo privacy, biometric consent, ecommerce personalization, and software reviews.

This replaces the abandoned directory submission approach. The new campaign is built around a real research page and a downloadable CSV, so the link request is about citing data rather than asking for a generic listing.

## First Principles

Backlinks appear when another site has a reason to make its page better by citing yours. The strongest reasons are:

1. The target page already discusses the same problem.
2. Your page gives them a concise fact, table, or source they do not already have.
3. The claim is verifiable and low risk for an editor to cite.
4. The outreach asks for a specific improvement to their existing article.
5. The anchor text looks natural and varied.

## Asset Created

The campaign asset is the Virtual Makeup Privacy Index 2026. It compares public disclosures across 10 beauty AI and virtual try-on tools on five policy checks:

1. Photo or face data processing.
2. Retention timing or on-device processing.
3. Deletion or privacy request path.
4. AI training, model improvement, or research disclosure.
5. Third-party processing or no-server processing disclosure.

Important caveat for outreach: this is a public-policy transparency index, not a backend security audit and not a legal conclusion.

## Anchor Strategy

Use natural anchors only. Do not repeat exact-match commercial anchors.

Recommended mix:

- 45 percent branded: AI Beauty Stylist.
- 25 percent source or page title: Virtual Makeup Privacy Index 2026.
- 20 percent naked URL.
- 10 percent contextual long-tail: virtual makeup privacy index, AI beauty app privacy data, virtual try-on privacy disclosures.

Avoid:

- best AI makeup app
- free AI makeup try on
- AI makeup generator
- exact commercial anchors repeated across placements

## Outreach Cadence

Day 1 to 2:

- Pitch the first 30 P1 and high-fit P2 targets.
- Personalize the first sentence with the target article or beat.
- Use one link only: the research page.

Day 4:

- Follow up once with a shorter note and the CSV link.

Day 7 to 10:

- Pitch the next 40 targets based on reply themes and opened opportunities.

Day 14:

- Pitch remaining qualified targets and review platforms only after at least a few editorial links or mentions exist.

## Quality Rules

Do:

- Pitch page updates where the article already mentions virtual try-on, AI photo editing, face data, biometric privacy, or beauty app reviews.
- Ask for a citation only when the index adds evidence to their article.
- Keep the link target to the research page, not the home page.
- Record every sent pitch, response, link, and rejection.

Do not:

- Buy links.
- Use PBNs.
- Mass-submit to generic AI directories.
- Use comment spam or forum profile links.
- Send the same exact pitch to 100 sites.
- Promise legal conclusions or safety rankings that the index does not prove.
