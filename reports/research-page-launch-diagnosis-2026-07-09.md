# Research Page Launch Diagnosis

Date: 2026-07-09
Page: https://aibeautystylist.com/research/virtual-makeup-privacy-index
Asset: Virtual Makeup Privacy Index 2026

## Verdict

The page is technically launchable, but the first shipped version is not yet strong enough for high-value outreach.

Technical acceptance: pass.

Editorial outreach acceptance before redesign: conditional fail.

## What Passed

- Live page and CSV exist.
- The page has a clear canonical research URL.
- Dataset schema and article schema are present.
- The CSV gives journalists a source they can inspect.
- Sitemap and footer discovery were added.
- The topic is timely: virtual try-on privacy, BIPA litigation, AI photo uploads, and disclosure quality are already being covered by beauty trade, legal, privacy, and tech media.

## What Was Not Strong Enough

- The page felt closer to a marketing landing page than a report.
- The hero did not immediately communicate research authority, review date, methodology, and citation value.
- The summary cards were useful but too generic for editors.
- The score table was functional, but did not look like a polished index or benchmark.
- The methodology needed clearer boundaries so readers know this is a public-disclosure review, not a backend security audit.
- The page needed stronger "cite this" and correction/update signals.
- The page needed a better mobile reading path for the full table.

## Acceptance Bar For Outreach

Before sending the first 30 pitches, the page should satisfy these conditions:

- Looks like a report or data brief within the first viewport.
- Shows the review date, sample size, checks, CSV, and caveat without hunting.
- Has an executive summary that can be quoted or paraphrased.
- Makes the scoring method obvious.
- Lets a reader scan the index without the table feeling raw.
- Includes source links, correction language, and non-legal disclaimer.
- Avoids exaggerated claims such as "safest" or "best privacy" unless independently proven.

## First 30 Outreach Gate

Do not send broad directory-style submissions from this asset. The correct first move is targeted article-update outreach:

- Beauty trade outlets that already wrote about MAC, Shiseido, Sephora, Clarins, AI beauty, or virtual try-on.
- Legal and privacy publications that already explain BIPA, biometric consent, virtual try-on, or photo retention.
- Tech outlets covering AI image privacy and consumer photo uploads.
- Ecommerce resources only when the article discusses virtual fitting rooms, AI shopping, merchant trust, or implementation risk.

The goal is to make the editor's existing page better with one cited dataset, not to ask for a generic backlink.
